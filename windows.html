HUFC Season Planner

For Windows · Heidelberg United FC

Install

  1. Open your Downloads folder, right-click HUFC Season Planner.zip and choose Extract All… → Extract.
  2. In the new folder, double-click Install HUFC Season Planner. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. The planner opens and puts a shortcut on your desktop. Sign in with the email you gave the club.

After this it updates itself — you never need to download it again. On an iPhone or iPad, use the app link instead: open the planner in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
