HUFC Season Planner — Privacy

Heidelberg United FC

The HUFC Season Planner is a coaching tool used by Heidelberg United FC's coaches to plan training sessions, matchday lineups and pre-game plans.

What the planner keeps

  • The sessions, lineups, pre-game plans and practices that coaches make, and the age groups they coach.
  • A coach's name and email address, so they can sign in and other coaches can see who made a plan.

This is kept on the coach's own devices and in the club's planner account (Google Firebase), and is shared only with the club's approved coaches.

Google Drive

A coach can choose to connect their Google Drive so that videos they add to pre-game plans can be watched on any device. When they do:

  • The planner only asks for the drive.file permission. It can only see and change the files it puts in the coach's Drive itself — nothing else in their Drive.
  • Videos go into a folder called “HUFC Planner clips” in the coach's own Drive, shared as “anyone with the link can view” so the club's coaches can play them in the planner.
  • The planner also reads the coach's Google email address, only to show which account is connected.
  • Google data is used only to store and play these videos. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.
  • A coach can disconnect at any time in the planner (Settings → Google Drive), or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The videos stay in their Drive until they delete them.

The planner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
